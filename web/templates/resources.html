Why an input can still say DATA_NEEDED

Connection or refresh needed

A feed may be blocked, delayed or waiting for its next refresh. Broker quotes and option chains can require a current Upstox login. Non-F&O stocks do not have an exchange option chain.

Company filing detail needed

A headline or notice link does not supply a verified financial line item. R&D, ESOP dilution, geographic revenue and one-off earnings adjustments need the relevant filing, period, units and attribution.

More historical observations needed

Indicators require their full price or quarterly history. New listings, missing sessions and newly connected feeds need enough valid observations before those calculations can run.

Calculation or research still pending

NOT COMPUTED can mean a scan is running or a calculation budget was reached. Some register items need a defined series, licensed data or a validated model. Connecting a general news feed does not resolve those requirements.
