ℹ️ What is "Engine Regime"?

Markets behave very differently in trends vs corrections. A momentum strategy that prints money in a bull run will get carved up in a panic. So AM07 runs two engines and a classifier decides which one fires entries today.

  • 🏔️ Everest (momentum/breakout) — active when Nifty is above its 200-day MA and FII flows are positive. Buys breakouts in leading sectors.
  • 🌊 Ocean (deep-reversion) — active when Nifty is >10% off its 52-week high. Buys quality names that have been heavily oversold (RSI <30 on weekly + climax volume).

The classifier reads four cheap inputs every 15 minutes:

  1. Nifty close vs 200-day moving average
  2. % below 52-week high
  3. India VIX level
  4. 5-day net FII flow (₹ Cr)

And lands on one of four regimes — bull_extension, neutral, correction, or panic — which also drives your dynamic cash reserve (20% / 25% / 35% / 50% respectively).
