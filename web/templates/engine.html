ℹ️ What is "Engine Regime"?

Markets behave very differently in trends vs corrections. A momentum strategy that prints money in a bull run will get carved up in a panic. So AM07 runs two engines and a classifier decides which one fires entries today.

  • 🏔️ Everest (momentum/breakout) — active when Nifty is above its 200-day MA (FII flow is shown for context, it does not gate). Buys breakouts in leading sectors.
  • 🌊 Ocean (deep-reversion) — the regime marks it eligible in a correction (Nifty below its 200-DMA or ≥12% off the 52-week high), panic (≥10% off + VIX>25), or neutral — but it only ever buys if your Ocean switch in Settings is ON. It is currently {% if ocean_enabled %}ON behind the crash guard (owner approval 2026-08-26): ₹1,68,750 per dip trade (your order 2026-08-30), max 8 slots, and four rails — VIX knife, damage cluster against the panel's own top-10% line (since 2026-10-04; the fixed 0.213 was on 84% of days), per-stock reclaim, danger veto — each block journaled. Measured honestly: these rails make Ocean rare — expect few or no Ocean buys outside a real flush{% else %}PARKED (off), so in a correction the app deliberately buys nothing until the VIX re-entry fires{% endif %}.

The classifier reads four cheap inputs on each refresh:

  1. Nifty close vs 200-day moving average
  2. % below 52-week high
  3. India VIX level
  4. 5-day net FII flow (₹ Cr)

And lands on one of four regimes — bull_extension, neutral, correction, or panic — which also drives your dynamic cash reserve (0% / 0% / 20% / 40% respectively — fully deployed in bull/neutral, protective cash forced in during correction/panic).
