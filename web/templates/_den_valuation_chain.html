stages 07-18 · computed from the committed statements, each row kept {{ chain.row_ttl_min }} min · no entry gate reads this; a judged base sets the EXIT target (item 19, below)
What it does. For each name below: locks the latest audited year (period end, publication date, currency — the US-dollar trap for ADR names is converted at the period-end rate and labelled, never silently), picks the valuation route from the sector-method table (unmapped names read UNDECLARED, never forced), reconciles the share count, strips one-offs from reported profit (checked against the audited normalised line), scores earnings quality, runs your Tata Tech scenario model as three cases (downside / base / upside) and prints the justified range per route, then the verdict: attractive · watch · unattractive · insufficient. Not a buy permission — the engine still buys on its own scores (the momentum blend for momentum, the AM07 composite for the other four categories); nothing here is read by an entry gate, a stop or a size. It does set the EXIT target since 2026-10-21 (item 19, your order "Wire A3" + "Exit as tested") — the block below.
Coverage. Statements exist for the {{ chain.n_statements }} Nifty-200 names fetched 2026-08-09 (+ the monthly refresh); any other name reads DATA_NEEDED until the batch fetch you have not yet approved runs. Peer multiples are derived: the median of the same route among those names (ASSUMED) — a peer list you type in the inputs table replaces it for that stock. Fed since 2026-10-21, each dated on its row: the 10-year G-sec yield (Upstox), the RBI repo rate and USD/INR (RBI, stored daily), FII + DII holding per stock. Rows of your P/E bridge the app does not feed — CPI, GDP, Brent (your choice), sentiment, chart score, delivery %, a repo start the RBI pages cannot verify (plus the Sensex / Nifty legs or USDINR when their stored levels are missing) — count 0 and are listed on each row.
Own target and market-adjusted target (2026-10-21). Every row prints both. The market-adjusted target = own target × (1 + β × the Sensex move since the selection date) × (1 + the sector index move − the Sensex move) × the rates leg × the currency leg through EPS — the legs split so nothing is counted twice; β and the sector coefficient are shown measured (252 sessions) and as your typed β = 1 / 1:1. The selection date is the time of action: a date you type in the inputs table, else your §11 target's date for that stock (typed there or pushed with the button below), else the results publication date (period end + 45 days, ASSUMED); the own target's P/E bridge reads the market only up to that date, so the move since then is counted once, in the market-adjusted target. The US indices (S&P 500, Nasdaq for IT, Dow) are printed with the part the Sensex already carries and counted 0. The currency leg runs through EPS for every sector (USD + EUR; shares typed → filed split → BRSR exports → your sector default → DATA_NEEDED, each row names its tier); it replaces the old IT-only rupee row E101 here — your Tata sheet keeps its own. "Verdict now" reads the market-adjusted target as of now; the own-target verdict prints beside it. Beside the target: the sector's total market cap and aggregate multiple against its 4-year FY-end average and its 52-week high, with NSE's own published sector P/E / P/B (dated) — these move no target.
