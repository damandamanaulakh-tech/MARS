Why v2 is not simply better

v2 maximises the growth rate. Growth rate and ruin risk climb together: concentration plus leverage plus full-Kelly drives the chance of a devastating drawdown toward certainty over enough bets — the account reaches zero and compounding ends. v1 accepts a lower ceiling to keep the floor above zero. That is the entire trade, and it is why the live engine is v1.

The trap detector, and why it lives here

v2's hunting ground — thin, under-covered, fast-moving names — is exactly where engineered pumps look for buyers. The flags above read that signature from public daily bars (parabolic move with no volume, ramps in stocks under ₹1 Cr/day turnover, volume spikes with price stalling, repeated near-limit-up days, no down days). Two flags and v2 refuses the name. Same knowledge, pointed at not being the exit liquidity.
