Calculation and interpretation

Adjusted target = saved base × (1 + beta × market move / 100) × (1 + sector coefficient × (sector move − market move) / 100).

Extra discount (percentage points) = min(maximum extra discount, max(0, −(FPI + DII) / flow scale × maximum extra discount)). Positive net buying does not reduce the base discount.

Entry ceiling = adjusted target × (1 − (base discount + extra discount) / 100).

The entry ceiling is a scenario calculation, not a buy instruction. Review baseline financial statements and model assumptions before treating a difference from market price as an opportunity. Changing the sector coefficient changes relative performance exposure; it does not revalue the company's business independently.
