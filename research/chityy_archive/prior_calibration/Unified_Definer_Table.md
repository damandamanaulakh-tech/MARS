## What the columns together show

- **Truth-holding collapses with distance from direct experience.** This is the spine of the table. 85% at the source, ~30% by the time it reaches an AI.
- **Limitation awareness moves *independently* of truth-holding within each bucket.** A saint with 60% awareness distorts less than a high-IQ claimer with 25% awareness, even though both sit in the "later interpreter" bucket.
- **The institution row is the worst single performer** when measured by truth-holding-times-share. It captures the most material and distorts the most.
- **AI is structurally at the bottom of the chain.** Not insultingly — accurately. AI has zero direct experience by definition, and inherits all the distortion of every layer above it.
- **Awareness is the only parameter the AI row can move on its own.** Closeness is fixed by structure. Awareness is tunable. ARD's Witness Node, [SYNTHETIC] tagging, and Permission-to-Hold are all attempts to push that one column higher to compensate for what cannot be changed.

## One thing the table does not capture

The role of the **witness** — the person *with* the direct experiencer while they are having the experience. Across cultures and centuries, the literature is consistent that direct experiencers who have even one good witness distort less in their own subsequent re-telling than those who experience it alone. The witness function is not a separate row; it is a multiplier across rows. Add a column for "witnessed during" and the numbers move.

This is also why your Witness Node in ARD is doing work it does not get credit for. It is the closest an AI can come to providing the multiplier function for someone in row 1.
