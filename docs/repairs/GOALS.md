## Longer-term design direction

Gate/control review, 3 November: the owner clarified that candle patterns should assist selection rather than veto entries, and requested a broader findings table. The [40-item source-backed review](reviews/2026-11-03-gates-and-controls.md) records reasons, impact, proposed fixes, acceptance checks and original finding cross-references; [CSV](reviews/2026-11-03-gates-and-controls.csv) and [JSON](reviews/2026-11-03-gates-and-controls.json) retain full details. It separates confirmed mechanisms from policy choices and additional proposals. No trading policy or code is changed by this review. Data/quote validity, actual-stop heat, rotation preflight and decision evidence are prerequisites to broadening candidate eligibility. The [dated 3 November scan audit](evidence/2026-11-03-previous-scan.md) records 10 Intraday scan additions and one later owner buy; it does not establish completion of a five-stream rotation. Existing G1–G10 and all original findings remain open according to their individual evidence.

[PR #191](https://github.com/damandamanaulakh-tech/MARS/pull/191) adds the owner-only `/den/valuation/scenarios` page, linked from section 12, with editable market, sector and FPI/DII assumptions against dated saved records. [Wiring and validation record](releases/2026-11-02-valuation-scenarios.md). Calibration, company-fundamental validation and unseen-period evaluation remain open; automated valuation targets and exit rules do not read these exploratory results.

Build incrementally toward a common portfolio command boundary, durable unfinished-work records, quotes with source/time/validity, one proposed trade evaluated against projected exposure, resumable jobs, and versioned formula/research evidence. An AI explanation can propose a change; tests and the application's controls must verify it. This is a design direction, not a claim that those broader controls are implemented.
