## Fresh held-out research

[The study and full comparison table](../../../research/sizing_2026_11_16/README.md)
retain protocol, runtime, universe, raw provider responses, hashes, every prior
sample, sizing reason, outcome and attribution. Protocol committed before prices:
`7bdb92fb0923467b8ca6939e7269fef27a7037e9`. Runner, training choice and outcomes
committed before the first holdout request:
`cd0eb2d8355494b81c91a2eb250eb5c7f37bd154`.

Fifteen different symbols were selected by a deterministic, price-independent
rule from the archived Nifty200 list. Train: 3–28 August; validation: September;
holdout: 1 October–13 November 2026. All 75 requests succeeded: 105,960 raw five-minute
bars, 105,750 accepted bars in 1,410 complete regular stock-sessions. The 210 bars
from the unverified Sunday special session on 8 November are explicitly excluded.
There are no missing regular-session outcome exclusions.

Holdout access was marked at 14:53:59.748424 UTC on 16 November before the first
request. There is no symbol/timestamp overlap with Goals08/09. Other stocks from
this historical market period had already been studied; this is a new historical
panel, not new forward evidence. Archived constituent membership, provider
revisions, fixed clocks and correlated daily outcomes limit generalization.

Candidates compare the ₹150,000 minimum ticket with a ₹2,500 net initial-stop risk
budget, ₹300,000 notional ceiling, prior measured net performance and a five-session
₹12,500 loss budget. Sizing uses completed prior-day reference outcomes only; every
net exit reason and both order charges count. Counterfactual shadow exits are
labelled and never claimed as actual candidate fills. A minimum ticket that does
not fit the risk budget is rejected. Prices, stops, targets and execution
assumptions are matched across candidates. All 5/15/30 bps per-side results and
both OHLC paths plus 30-minute observation scenarios are retained.

The locked `measured_stop_risk` candidate made **zero of 435** holdout trades in
every scenario. At 5 bps/side with 30-minute observations it withheld ₹399,401.08 of
losing reference outcomes but missed ₹205,222.64 of positive outcomes. Its ₹194,178.44
paired benefit came from staying in cash; expectancy is unknown, and the required
100 executed trades are absent. **No experimental sizing or loss policy is promoted.**

The loss-budget diagnostic made 133 trades and still lost ₹50,370.33 after costs;
the matched control lost ₹194,178.44. Net-stop sizing alone lost ₹202,568.90. Under
30-minute observation, 65 control/stop-risk trades lost more than planned initial
stop risk. Correct quantity does not guarantee timely protective execution.
Actual strategy replay, forward paper outcomes and owner promotion review remain
in SEQ-11; none is replaced by this bounded comparison.
