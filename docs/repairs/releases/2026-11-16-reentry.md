## Source behavior and bounded implementation

Baseline `9ba36523fe0df77adbee49dbe40a6201118db4b5` checks category loss halts,
existing holdings, completed intraday data and final entry validity, but does
not require a post-loss setup transition. The prior Momentum-only cooldown
study performed worse on its holdout; it is not justification for a new
intraday waiting threshold.

[The Goal09 study](../../../research/reentry_2026_11_16/README.md) implements
three causal changed-setup candidates: post-loss breakout reclaim, session-VWAP
reclaim with two confirmed closes, and both requirements. Each requires an
observed reset then reclaim in complete bars after the loss is known. It
records values, thresholds, witness bars, decision times, cutoff and the first
eligible retry. Same-bar rebounds, old crossings and score reuse cannot pass.
Time-only waiting and no-retry policies remain diagnostic controls.

Each policy receives the same failed initial trade within a fill/slippage
scenario and makes at most one subsequent trade. Entries at 10:00/13:00 and
retry clocks are fixed; this is not replay of actual MARS picks or all repeated
trades. Protective price exits and 5m-ATR targets retain their current formulas.
There is no settings, order, portfolio or ledger write operation.

At 30-minute observations and 5 bps adverse slippage per side, 104 common
failed-seed episodes had a later retry opportunity over 34 days and 15 names.

Attribution is exact: **paired gain = losses withheld − recoveries missed +
delayed retry net P&L**. A delayed trade can add a fresh loss. The selected
rule's two actual retries were HINDUNILVR on 21 April (+INR2,037.25) and ONGC
on 2 June (-INR12.89). Common initial losses are not erased: seed-plus-retry
P&L is -INR132,826.16 for these episodes.

The paired mean improvement is 29.813 bps of the fixed INR100,000 budget,
95% whole-day bootstrap interval [8.069, 57.771] bps. All nine paired intervals
are positive. That establishes a difference in this modeled retry comparison,
but not a profitable recovery strategy: the candidate almost never trades at
30-minute cadence and its retry P&L is negative under both OHLC orderings.

The frozen sample floors were not met in all strata: 150 holdout pairs and
50 candidate retries, plus minimum training/validation days with episodes.
Validation had no breakout-reclaim retries at 30-minute cadence. Sparse samples
and failed cross-scenario expectancy are rejection reasons, not skipped checks.

retain 5/15/30 bps sensitivity, counts, gross/fees/slippage, payoff, defined
